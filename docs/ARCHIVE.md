## Products

Crafty Machine invests in new skills and technologies by launching products internally. We have built a range of products to solve problems from contractor and employee management to SEO tracking to keeping the streets safe for bicyclists and pedestrians. Start work with Crafty Machine today to put your business in position to create the next wave in human-collaboration software interfaces, create value in your business, leapfrog your competitors and unlock more efficient cost-structures in your industry.

HaveMyShft

Crafty Machine has taken a lead role in developing and commercializing HaveMyShift. HaveMyShift is a platform for hourly workers to swap shifts with each other, giving employees access to more hours, more time off, and crucial flexibility when emergencies happen. HaveMyShift was a predecessor and thought leader for “on-demand” workforces technologies like Uber.

Fresh Metrix

Crafty Machine launched Fresh Metrix to address an opportunity in the market for SEO tools. The MVP was completed in a span of weeks, allowing the team to validate market position and value propositions with minimal time and money spent. Development took place in Medellin, Colombia, where Crafty Machine maintains close ties with the entrepreneurship and development communities.

Cycle Voice

Cycle Voice is a mobile application designed to increase bicycle and pedestrian safety by removing some of the anonymity inherent to driving a motor vehicle. The technology behind this product allows individuals to create quick and accurate records of dangerous driving behavior and makes those records available to municipalities, professional drivers, and automobile insurance agencies. Drivers who have endangered the personal safety of cyclists and pedestrians are sent friendly notices asking them to familiarize themselves with traffic safety laws and the risks they take on when operating a motor vehicle.

Zee Manager

Zee Manager brings artificial intelligence to the worlds of task management and project planning. Some of the work inherent in organizing a project can be offloaded to a virtual manager who keeps workers on track and on budget, and will bring a human manager in to the discussion as needed.
